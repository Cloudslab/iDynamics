# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- focus criticism on technical content and reproducibility;
- welcome different levels of experience and research background;
- avoid harassment, insults, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- respect confidentiality when handling vulnerabilities, unpublished data, or
  access-controlled infrastructure material.

Unacceptable behavior includes sustained disruption, personal attacks,
harassment, publishing another person's sensitive information without consent,
or knowingly encouraging unsafe use of live-cluster tooling.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, limit
participation, or block access for conduct that violates these standards.
Reports should be sent through the repository host's maintainer contact or
moderation channels. Include enough context for review, but do not post
sensitive security details or access material in public threads.
